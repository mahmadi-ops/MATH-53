How to read this figure
  1. Each equation is a line, and a solution of the system is a point lying on both lines.
  2. The slider for a turns the mustard line about the point (0, b), where it crosses the y-axis: its slope is −a. The slider for b moves the line up and down without turning it.
  3. Subtracting the first equation from the second gives (a − 1)x = b − 1. The readout shows this equation for the current a and b; everything depends on whether a − 1 is zero.
  4. Move a toward 1 from either side and watch the crossing point run off the window. At a = 1 exactly, there is no crossing point at all, unless b = 1.
