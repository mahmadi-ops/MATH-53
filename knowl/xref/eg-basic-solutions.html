Example 2.21 Basic Solutions of a Homogeneous System.

Continuing Example 2.19, write the general solution as a linear combination.
🔗
Solution.
Renaming the parameters \(a_1\) and \(a_2\text{,}\) the general solution is
\begin{equation*} \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3 \\ x_4 \end{bmatrix} = a_1 \begin{bmatrix} \frac{9}{5} \\ -\frac{4}{5} \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} + a_2 \begin{bmatrix} -\frac{14}{5} \\ -\frac{1}{5} \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} = a_1 \vec{v}_1 + a_2 \vec{v}_2. \end{equation*}
🔗
The vectors
\begin{equation*} \vec{v}_1 = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{9}{5} \\ -\frac{4}{5} \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} \qquad \text{and} \qquad \vec{v}_2 = \begin{bmatrix} -\frac{14}{5} \\ -\frac{1}{5} \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
are the basic solutions, and the general solution \(\vec{v}_h = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3 \\ x_4 \end{bmatrix}\) is a linear combination of the basic ones.
