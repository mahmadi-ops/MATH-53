Definition 6.27. Dot Product, Length, and Angle.

For \(\vec{u} = \begin{bmatrix} u_1 \\ u_2 \\ u_3 \end{bmatrix}\) and \(\vec{v} = \begin{bmatrix} v_1 \\ v_2 \\ v_3 \end{bmatrix}\) in \(\R^3\text{,}\) the dot product and the length (or norm) are
\begin{equation*} \vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = u_1v_1 + u_2v_2 + u_3v_3, \qquad \| \vec{u} \| = \sqrt{\vec{u} \cdot \vec{u}}, \end{equation*}
and the angle \(\theta \in [0, \pi]\) between two nonzero vectors is determined by \(\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \| \vec{u} \| \| \vec{v} \| \cos\theta\text{.}\)
