Exercise 2. ★ Computing a Minor and a Cofactor.

Let \(A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp 1 \amp 0 \\ 1 \amp 3 \amp -1 \\ 0 \amp 2 \amp 1 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\) Compute \(\operatorname{minor}(A)_{32}\) and \(C_{32}\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
For a \(3 \times 3\) matrix the signs alternate as
\begin{equation*} \begin{bmatrix} + \amp - \amp + \\ - \amp + \amp - \\ + \amp - \amp + \end{bmatrix}, \end{equation*}
so position \((3,2)\) carries a minus sign.
🔗
Deleting row \(3\) and column \(2\) leaves
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{minor}(A)_{32} = \begin{vmatrix} 2 \amp 0 \\ 1 \amp -1 \end{vmatrix} = -2 - 0 = -2, \end{equation*}
and therefore
\begin{equation*} C_{32} = (-1)^{3+2} \operatorname{minor}(A)_{32} = (-1)^5(-2) = 2. \end{equation*}
