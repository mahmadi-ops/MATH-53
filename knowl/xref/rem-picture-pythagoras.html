Remark 7.57. Pythagoras for Pictures.

If \(A \cdot B = 0\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} \| A + B \|^2 = \| A \|^2 + \| B \|^2. \end{equation*}
Read (7.1) with \(-B\) in place of \(Y\text{:}\) it becomes \(\| A + B \|^2 = \| A \|^2 + 2\, A \cdot B + \| B \|^2\text{,}\) and the middle term is zero by assumption. The \(3\)-\(4\)-\(5\) triangle of The Distance Between Two Pictures was this identity in the \(2 \times 2\) case.
