Definition 7.18. Coordinate Vector and Coordinate Mapping.

The coordinates of \(\vec{x}\) relative to the basis \(\mathcal{B}\) are denoted by
\begin{equation*} [\vec{x}]_{\mathcal{B}} = \begin{bmatrix} c_1 \\ c_2 \\ \vdots \\ c_n \end{bmatrix}, \end{equation*}
and the mapping \(\vec{x} \mapsto [\vec{x}]_{\mathcal{B}}\) is known as the coordinate mapping.
