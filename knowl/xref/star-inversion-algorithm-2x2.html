Exercise 33. ★ The Algorithm on a \(2 \times 2\) Matrix.

Let \(A = \begin{bmatrix} 3 \amp -8 \\ 2 \amp -5 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\) Find \(A^{-1}\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Augment with \(I_2\) and row reduce:
\begin{align*} \left[ \, A \mid I_2 \, \right] \amp = \left[ \begin{array}{cc|cc} 3 \amp -8 \amp 1 \amp 0 \\ 2 \amp -5 \amp 0 \amp 1 \end{array} \right]\\ \amp \xrightarrow{-r_2 + r_1} \left[ \begin{array}{cc|cc} 1 \amp -3 \amp 1 \amp -1 \\ 2 \amp -5 \amp 0 \amp 1 \end{array} \right]\\ \amp \xrightarrow{-2r_1 + r_2} \left[ \begin{array}{cc|cc} 1 \amp -3 \amp 1 \amp -1 \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp -2 \amp 3 \end{array} \right]\\ \amp \xrightarrow{3r_2 + r_1} \left[ \begin{array}{cc|cc} 1 \amp 0 \amp -5 \amp 8 \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp -2 \amp 3 \end{array} \right]. \end{align*}
Therefore
\begin{equation*} A^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} -5 \amp 8 \\ -2 \amp 3 \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
🔗
For a \(2 \times 2\) matrix the formula of Theorem 4.36 is quicker. Here \(ad - bc = (3)(-5) - (-8)(2) = -15 + 16 = 1\text{,}\) so
\begin{equation*} A^{-1} = \frac{1}{1}\begin{bmatrix} -5 \amp 8 \\ -2 \amp 3 \end{bmatrix}, \end{equation*}
in agreement with the algorithm.
