Example 2.14 Writing a System in Matrix Form.

Express the system of Example 2.12 in matrix form.
🔗
Solution.
The system is \(A\vec{X} = \vec{b}\) with
\begin{equation*} A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \amp 4 \amp -3 \\ 0 \amp -1 \amp 5 \\ 1 \amp 1 \amp 4 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \vec{X} = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \vec{b} = \begin{bmatrix} -6 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
Nothing new has to be computed. The columns \(\vec{A}_1, \vec{A}_2, \vec{A}_3\) of \(A\) are the three vectors of the vector form in Example 2.12, and by Definition 2.13
\begin{equation*} A\vec{X} = x_1 \vec{A}_1 + x_2 \vec{A}_2 + x_3 \vec{A}_3. \end{equation*}
