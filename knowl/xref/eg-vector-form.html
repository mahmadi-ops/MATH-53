Example 2.12. Writing a System in Vector Form.

Express the following system of linear equations in vector form:
\begin{align*} 2x_1 + 4x_2 - 3x_3 \amp = -6\\ \phantom{2x_1 + {}} -x_2 + 5x_3 \amp = 0\\ x_1 + x_2 + 4x_3 \amp = 1 \end{align*}
🔗
Solution.
\begin{equation*} x_1 \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} + x_2 \begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} + x_3 \begin{bmatrix} -3 \\ 5 \\ 4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -6 \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
