Remark 7.49. What Else Rides Across.

The dot product is not a special case. Any notion that Definition 6.27 settles by a formula in the coordinates of a vector—not only the dot product, but perpendicularity and angle as well—rides to pictures along the isomorphism of Remark 7.14 in exactly the way Definition 7.47 has just carried it: read the picture through its pixel entries and run the formula. This section will cash that promise twice more, once for perpendicular pictures in Perpendicular Pictures and What the Score Ignores and once for the angle between two pictures in What the Method Does Not See.
