Example 7.3. The Vector Space of \(2 \times 2\) Matrices.

Let \(\mathbb{M}_{22}\) be the set of all \(2 \times 2\) matrices. Is \(\mathbb{M}_{22}\) a vector space?
🔗
Solution.
Yes. Let \(A, B, C \in \mathbb{M}_{22}\text{,}\) and let \(a\) and \(b \in \R\text{.}\)
🔗
  • (I) \(A + B \in \mathbb{M}_{22}\text{,}\) since the sum of two \(2 \times 2\) matrices is again \(2 \times 2\text{.}\)
