Exercise 12. ★ A Change of Basis in \(\mathbb{P}_2\).

Let \(\mathcal{B}_1 = \{1, x, x^2\}\) and \(\mathcal{B}_2 = \{1, \; 1 + x, \; 1 + x + x^2\}\) be ordered bases of \(\mathbb{P}_2\text{.}\) Find \(P_{\mathcal{B}_2 \leftarrow \mathcal{B}_1}\) and use it to find \([p]_{\mathcal{B}_2}\) for \(p(x) = 2 + 3x - x^2\text{.}\)
