Example 9.9. Stochastic Matrices Preserve Sums.

Show that stochastic matrices (as defined above) preserve the sum of entries when acting on vectors in \(\R^n\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Assume \(T\) is a stochastic matrix and \(\vec{x} = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_n \end{bmatrix} \in \R^n\text{.}\) Then the \(i\)th entry of \(T\vec{x}\) is
\begin{equation*} \left( T\vec{x} \right)_i = \sum_{j=1}^{n} T_{ij} x_j, \end{equation*}
and so
\begin{equation*} \text{sum of entries of } T\vec{x} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} T_{ij} x_j = \sum_{j=1}^{n} \underbrace{\left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} T_{ij} \right)}_{= \, 1} x_j = \sum_{j=1}^{n} x_j, \end{equation*}
the sum of entries of \(\vec{x}\text{.}\) The inner sum equals one since \(T\) is stochastic.
