Example 3.52. Reflection through the Origin.

Reflection through the origin sends \(\vec{x} = \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix}\) to \(T(\vec{x}) = \begin{bmatrix} -x \\ -y \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) so
\begin{equation*} A = \begin{bmatrix} -1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp -1 \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
This can also be thought of as \(R_\pi\text{,}\) since
\begin{equation*} R_\pi = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\pi \amp -\sin\pi \\ \sin\pi \amp \cos\pi \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp -1 \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
