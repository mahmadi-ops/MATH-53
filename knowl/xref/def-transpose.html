Definition 4.23. Transpose.

If \(A\) is an \(m \times n\) matrix, then its transpose, denoted \(A^T\text{,}\) is the \(n \times m\) matrix whose \(i\)th row is the \(i\)th column of \(A\text{.}\) That is, if \(A = \begin{bmatrix} a_{ij} \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) then
\begin{equation*} A^T = \begin{bmatrix} a_{ij} \end{bmatrix}^T = \begin{bmatrix} a_{ji} \end{bmatrix}, \end{equation*}
i.e., the \((i,j)\)-entry of \(A^T\) is the \((j,i)\)-entry of \(A\text{.}\)
