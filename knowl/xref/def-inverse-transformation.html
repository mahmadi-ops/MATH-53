Definition 4.53 Inverse of a Transformation.

Let \(T : \R^n \to \R^n\) be a transformation. A transformation \(S : \R^n \to \R^n\) is an inverse of \(T\) if, for every \(\vec{x} \in \R^n\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} S\left( T(\vec{x}) \right) = \vec{x} \qquad \text{and} \qquad T\left( S(\vec{x}) \right) = \vec{x}, \end{equation*}
that is, if \(S \circ T\) and \(T \circ S\) are both the identity transformation. In that case \(T\) is called invertible, and \(S\) is written \(T^{-1}\text{.}\)
