Definition 5.28 Kernel of a Transformation.

Let \(T : \R^n \to \R^m\) be a linear transformation. The kernel of \(T\) is the set of all inputs that \(T\) sends to the zero vector:
\begin{equation*} \operatorname{Ker} T = \left\{ \vec{x} \in \R^n \; : \; T(\vec{x}) = \vec{0} \right\}. \end{equation*}
