Lemma 6.31. The Cross Product is Perpendicular to Both Factors.

\(\vec{u} \cdot (\vec{u} \times \vec{v}) = 0\) and \(\vec{v} \cdot (\vec{u} \times \vec{v}) = 0\text{.}\) Hence, when \(\vec{n} = \vec{u} \times \vec{v} \neq \vec{0}\text{,}\) the vector \(\vec{n}\) is perpendicular to the entire plane \(\operatorname{Span}\{ \vec{u}, \vec{v} \}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Direct computation:
\begin{equation*} \vec{u} \cdot (\vec{u} \times \vec{v}) = u_1(u_2v_3 - u_3v_2) + u_2(u_3v_1 - u_1v_3) + u_3(u_1v_2 - u_2v_1) = 0 \end{equation*}
after cancellation in pairs, and symmetrically for \(\vec{v}\text{.}\) Any vector of the plane has the form \(s\vec{u} + t\vec{v}\text{,}\) and \((s\vec{u} + t\vec{v}) \cdot \vec{n} = s(\vec{u} \cdot \vec{n}) + t(\vec{v} \cdot \vec{n}) = 0\text{.}\)
