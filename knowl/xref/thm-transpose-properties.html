Theorem 4.25. Properties of the Transpose of a Matrix.

Let \(A\) and \(B\) be \(m \times n\) matrices, \(C\) be an \(n \times p\) matrix, and \(r \in \R\) a scalar. Then
🔗
  1. \(\left( A^T \right)^T = A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \((rA)^T = rA^T\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. \((A + B)^T = A^T + B^T\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. \((AC)^T = C^T A^T\text{.}\)

    🔗
