Theorem 6.11. Row Operations and Determinants.

Let \(A\) be an \(n \times n\) matrix.
🔗
  1. If \(B\) is obtained from \(A\) by exchanging two different rows of \(A\text{,}\) then \(\det B = -\det A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. If \(B\) is obtained from \(A\) by multiplying a row of \(A\) by a scalar \(k \in \R\text{,}\) then \(\det B = k \det A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. If \(B\) is obtained from \(A\) by adding \(k\) times one row of \(A\) to a different row of \(A\text{,}\) then \(\det B = \det A\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  4. If two different rows (or columns) of \(A\) are identical, then \(\det A = 0\text{.}\) This follows from (3).
