Example 2.8 Adding and Scaling Vectors.

Let
\begin{equation*} \vec{u} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \vec{v} = \begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ 5 \\ 6 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad k = -2. \end{equation*}
Addition and scalar multiplication both work entry by entry:
\begin{equation*} \vec{u} + \vec{v} = \begin{bmatrix} 5 \\ 7 \\ 9 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad k\vec{v} = -2 \begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ 5 \\ 6 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -8 \\ -10 \\ -12 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \vec{u} + k\vec{v} = \begin{bmatrix} -7 \\ -8 \\ -9 \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
