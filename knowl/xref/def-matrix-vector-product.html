Definition 2.13. The Product of a Matrix and a Vector.

Let \(A = \begin{bmatrix} a_{ij} \end{bmatrix}\) be an \(m \times n\) matrix with columns \(A_1, A_2, \ldots, A_n\text{,}\) written \(A = \begin{bmatrix} A_1 \amp A_2 \amp \cdots \amp A_n \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) and let \(X\) be an \(n \times 1\) column vector, \(X = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_n \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
Then the product of matrix \(A\) and (column) vector \(X\) is the \(m \times 1\) column vector given by
\begin{equation*} \begin{bmatrix} A_1 \amp A_2 \amp \cdots \amp A_n \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_n \end{bmatrix} = x_1 A_1 + x_2 A_2 + \cdots + x_n A_n = \sum_{j=1}^{n} x_j A_j; \end{equation*}
that is, \(AX\) is a linear combination of the columns of \(A\text{.}\)
