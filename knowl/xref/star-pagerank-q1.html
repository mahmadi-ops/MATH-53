Exercise 14. ★ Question 1.

A surfer starts by choosing one of the \(N = 3\) pages at random. Let \(p_i(t)\) be the probability that the surfer is on page \(i\) at time \(t\text{.}\) What are \(p_i(0)\) and \(p_i(t+1)\text{?}\)
🔗
Solution.
At time \(0\text{.}\) All three pages are equally likely, so
\begin{equation*} p_i(0) = \frac{1}{N} = \frac{1}{3} \qquad \text{for } i = 1, 2, 3. \end{equation*}
🔗
At time \(t+1\text{.}\) The surfer arrives at page \(i\) by having been on some page \(j\) that links to \(i\text{,}\) and then choosing the link to \(i\) from among the \(\ell_j\) outbound links of page \(j\text{.}\) So
\begin{equation*} p_i(t+1) = \sum_{j \to i} \frac{p_j(t)}{\ell_j}, \end{equation*}
where the sum runs over the pages \(j\) that link to \(i\) and \(\ell_j\) is the number of outbound links of page \(j\text{.}\)
🔗
Here \(\ell_1 = 2\text{,}\) \(\ell_2 = 1\text{,}\) \(\ell_3 = 1\text{.}\)
