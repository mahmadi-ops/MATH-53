Example 3.48. Reflection in the \(x\)-Axis.

In \(\R^2\text{,}\) reflection in the \(x\)-axis, which transforms \(\begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \end{bmatrix}\) to \(\begin{bmatrix} a \\ -b \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) is a matrix transformation. Since \(T(\vec{e}_1) = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}\) and \(T(\vec{e}_2) = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ -1 \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} T \begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \end{bmatrix} = a \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix} + b \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ -1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \\ 0 \amp -1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} a \\ b \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} a \\ -b \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
