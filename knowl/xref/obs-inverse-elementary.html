Observation 4.55.

For finding the inverse of an elementary matrix \(E\text{,}\) we can apply to \(I\) the inverse of the row operation applied to \(I\) to obtain \(E\text{.}\) This will be more efficient than the matrix inversion algorithm.
🔗
In particular, every elementary matrix is invertible, and its inverse is again an elementary matrix.
