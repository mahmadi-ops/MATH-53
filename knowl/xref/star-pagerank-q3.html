Exercise 16. ★ Question 3.

Find the matrix \(L\) representing the evolution of the probabilities from time \(t\) to time \(t+1\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Collect the three relations of Exercise 10.9.15 into \(\vec{p}(t+1) = L\vec{p}(t)\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} L = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \amp 0 \amp 1 \\ \frac{1}{2} \amp 0 \amp 0 \\ \frac{1}{2} \amp 1 \amp 0 \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
🔗
Column \(j\) records where a surfer on page \(j\) goes: column \(1\) splits \(\tfrac{1}{2}\) and \(\tfrac{1}{2}\) between pages \(2\) and \(3\text{;}\) columns \(2\) and \(3\) each send everything to a single page.
🔗
Every column sums to \(1\text{,}\) so \(L\) is stochastic (Definition 9.7) — as it must be, since the surfer certainly lands somewhere.
