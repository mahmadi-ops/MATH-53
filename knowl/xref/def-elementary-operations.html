Definition 1.18 Elementary Operations.

The following three operations on a system of linear equations are called elementary operations.
  1. Interchange: interchange two equations.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Scaling: multiply an equation by a nonzero number.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Replacement: replace an equation by the sum of itself and a multiple of a different equation. (Briefly: add a multiple of one equation to another.)
