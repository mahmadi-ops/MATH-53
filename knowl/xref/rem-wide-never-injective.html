Remark 3.24. Wide Matrices Are Never One-to-One.

If \(A\) is an \(m \times n\) matrix with more columns than rows (\(n \gt m\)), then \(A\) cannot have a pivot in every column: there is at most one pivot per row, and there are fewer rows than columns. Hence \(A\vec{x} = \vec{0}\) has a free variable, and the transformation \(T(\vec{x}) = A\vec{x}\) is never one-to-one. This is why the transformation \(T : \R^3 \to \R^2\) in the previous example had no chance: a transformation from a bigger space to a smaller space must collapse different inputs together.
