Definition 7.23. Change of Basis Matrix.

Let \(\mathcal{B}_1 = \{ \vec{u}_1, \vec{u}_2, \ldots, \vec{u}_n \}\) and \(\mathcal{B}_2 = \{ \vec{v}_1, \vec{v}_2, \ldots, \vec{v}_n \}\) be two bases of a vector space. Then to change the basis with respect to which the vector \(\vec{x}\) is written from \(\mathcal{B}_1\) to \(\mathcal{B}_2\text{,}\) we can use the change of basis matrix \(P_{\mathcal{B}_2 \leftarrow \mathcal{B}_1}\) as
\begin{equation*} P_{\mathcal{B}_2 \leftarrow \mathcal{B}_1} \, [\vec{x}]_{\mathcal{B}_1} = [\vec{x}]_{\mathcal{B}_2}, \end{equation*}
where
\begin{equation*} P_{\mathcal{B}_2 \leftarrow \mathcal{B}_1} = \begin{bmatrix} [\vec{u}_1]_{\mathcal{B}_2} \amp [\vec{u}_2]_{\mathcal{B}_2} \amp \cdots \amp [\vec{u}_n]_{\mathcal{B}_2} \end{bmatrix}. \end{equation*}
