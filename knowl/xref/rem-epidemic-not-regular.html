Remark 9.18. Why the Steady State Is Not Unique.

Does this contradict Theorem 9.12? No: that theorem assumes the transition matrix is regular, and \(T\) is not. However high the power \(T^k\text{,}\) its third and fourth columns remain \(\vec{x}_1\) and \(\vec{x}_2\) (recovered and deceased individuals never leave their states), so \(T^k\) always has zero entries. You can confirm this in Sage by evaluating, say, T^50.
