Theorem 6.14. Determinant of a Scalar Multiple.

If \(A\) is an \(n \times n\) matrix and \(k\) is any scalar, then
\begin{equation*} \det(kA) = k^n \det A. \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Multiplying \(A\) by \(k\) multiplies every one of the \(n\) rows by \(k\text{.}\) Applying part (2) of Theorem 6.11 once per row produces a factor of \(k\) each time.
