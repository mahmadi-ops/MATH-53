Exercise 7. ★ The Same Vector in Two Coordinate Systems.

Let \(\mathcal{B}_1 = \{\vec{e}_1, \vec{e}_2\}\) be the standard basis of \(\R^2\) and let \(\mathcal{B}_2 = \{\vec{b}_1, \vec{b}_2\}\) satisfy
\begin{equation*} \vec{e}_1 = \tfrac{1}{3}\vec{b}_1, \qquad \vec{e}_2 = \tfrac{1}{3}\vec{b}_1 + \vec{b}_2. \end{equation*}
If \([\vec{x}]_{\mathcal{B}_1} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\text{,}\) find \([\vec{x}]_{\mathcal{B}_2}\text{,}\) and find \(\vec{b}_1\) and \(\vec{b}_2\) explicitly.
