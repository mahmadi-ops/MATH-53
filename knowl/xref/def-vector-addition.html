Definition 2.4. Addition in \(\R^n\).

  • If \(\vec{u}\) and \(\vec{v}\) are in \(\R^n\text{,}\) then \(\vec{u} + \vec{v}\) is obtained by adding together corresponding entries of the vectors.
    🔗
    🔗
  • The zero vector in \(\R^n\) is the \(n \times 1\) zero matrix, and is denoted \(\vec{0}\text{.}\)
