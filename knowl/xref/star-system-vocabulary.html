Exercise 2. ★ The Parts of a System.

For the system
\begin{align*} x_1 + x_2 - 3x_3 \amp = 2\\ 2x_1 - x_2 + x_3 \amp = 1 \end{align*}
name the variables, the coefficients, and the constant terms.
🔗
Solution.
The variables are \(x_1\text{,}\) \(x_2\text{,}\) \(x_3\text{.}\)
🔗
The coefficients are the numbers multiplying the variables: \(1, 1, -3\) in the first equation and \(2, -1, 1\) in the second.
🔗
The constant terms are the numbers on the right-hand sides: \(2\) and \(1\text{.}\)
