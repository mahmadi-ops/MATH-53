Theorem 2.22. Particular Plus Homogeneous.

Suppose the matrix equation \(A\vec{x} = \vec{b}\) is consistent and let \(\vec{p} \neq \vec{0}\) be a solution. Assume that \(\vec{v}_h\) is a solution of \(A\vec{x} = \vec{0}\text{.}\) Then the solution set of \(A\vec{x} = \vec{b}\) is the set of all the vectors of the form \(\vec{w} = \vec{p} + \vec{v}_h\text{.}\)
