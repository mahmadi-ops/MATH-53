Definition 7.47. Dot Product, Length, and Distance for Pictures.

For pictures \(X = [x_{ij}]\) and \(Y = [y_{ij}]\) in \(\mathbb{M}_{mn}\text{,}\) the dot product, the length, and the distance are
\begin{equation*} X \cdot Y = \sum_{i,j} x_{ij}\, y_{ij}, \qquad \| X \| = \sqrt{X \cdot X}, \qquad \operatorname{dist}(X, Y) = \| X - Y \|. \end{equation*}
