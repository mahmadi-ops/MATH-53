Definition 7.11. Linear Transformation of Vector Spaces.

A linear transformation from a vector space \(V\) to another vector space \(W\) is a rule that assigns to each \(\vec{x} \in V\) a unique vector \(T(\vec{x}) \in W\) and it preserves vector addition and scalar multiplication.
🔗
Equivalently, for every \(\vec{x}_1\) and \(\vec{x}_2 \in V\) and \(a_1, a_2 \in \R\text{,}\) we have
\begin{equation*} T \left( a_1 \vec{x}_1 + a_2 \vec{x}_2 \right) = a_1 T(\vec{x}_1) + a_2 T(\vec{x}_2); \end{equation*}
that is, \(T\) preserves linear combinations.
