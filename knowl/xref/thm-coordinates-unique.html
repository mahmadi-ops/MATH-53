Theorem 7.17. Coordinates Are Unique.

Let \(\mathcal{B} = \{ \vec{b}_i \}_{i=1}^{n}\) be a basis for a vector space \(V\text{.}\) Then for each \(\vec{x} \in V\text{,}\) there exists a unique set of scalars \(\{ c_i \}_{i=1}^{n}\) such that
\begin{equation*} \vec{x} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} c_i \vec{b}_i. \end{equation*}
