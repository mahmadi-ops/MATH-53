Exercise 15. ★ Describing a Span.

Describe \(\operatorname{Span}\{\vec{u}, \vec{v}\}\text{,}\) where \(\vec{u} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}\) and \(\vec{v} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 0 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
Every linear combination has the form
\begin{equation*} a_1 \vec{u} + a_2 \vec{v} = a_1 \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} + a_2 \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 0 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} a_1 + 2a_2 \\ 0 \\ 2a_1 + 3a_2 \end{bmatrix}, \end{equation*}
whose middle entry is always \(0\text{.}\) So every such vector lies in the \(xz\)-plane.
🔗
Conversely, take an arbitrary vector \(\begin{bmatrix} x \\ 0 \\ z \end{bmatrix}\) of the \(xz\)-plane and solve \(x_1 \vec{u} + x_2 \vec{v} = \begin{bmatrix} x \\ 0 \\ z \end{bmatrix}\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \left[ \begin{array}{cc|c} 1 \amp 2 \amp x \\ 2 \amp 3 \amp z \end{array} \right] \longrightarrow \left[ \begin{array}{cc|c} 1 \amp 2 \amp x \\ 0 \amp -1 \amp z - 2x \end{array} \right] \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} \longrightarrow \left[ \begin{array}{cc|c} 1 \amp 0 \amp -3x + 2z \\ 0 \amp 1 \amp 2x - z \end{array} \right], \end{equation*}
which is consistent for every \(x\) and \(z\text{,}\) with \(x_1 = -3x + 2z\) and \(x_2 = 2x - z\text{.}\) That is,
\begin{equation*} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ 0 \\ z \end{bmatrix} = (-3x + 2z)\vec{u} + (2x - z)\vec{v}. \end{equation*}
🔗
Hence \(\operatorname{Span}\{\vec{u}, \vec{v}\}\) is exactly the \(xz\)-plane. As a check, \((1,0,0)\) should be \(-3\vec{u} + 2\vec{v}\text{,}\) and indeed \(-3(1,0,2) + 2(2,0,3) = (1,0,0)\text{.}\) \(\checkmark\)
