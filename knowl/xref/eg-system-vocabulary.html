Example 1.3 The Parts of a System.

The following is a system of linear equations:
\begin{align*} x_1 - 2x_2 - 7x_3 \amp = -1\\ -x_1 + 3x_2 + 6x_3 \amp = 0 \end{align*}
Its variables are \(x_1\text{,}\) \(x_2\text{,}\) \(x_3\text{;}\) its coefficients, the numbers multiplying the variables, are \(1, -2, -7\) in the first equation and \(-1, 3, 6\) in the second; and its constant terms, on the right-hand sides, are \(-1\) and \(0\text{.}\)
